## 2024-05-18 - [Arc vs Deep Cloning for State Trees]
**Learning:** Eagerly cloning large state trees like `SampleBank` (via `Arc::new(samples.clone())`) into evaluation closures and DSP boundaries causes significant O(N) allocation overhead per evaluation. Furthermore, performing equality checks on `Arc<T>` directly (`a != b`) performs a deep structural comparison.
**Action:** Lift `Arc<T>` storage to the parent (e.g., `ReplSession` and `EngineCore`) and pass `Arc::clone(&state)` to eliminate the deep copy. Use `!Arc::ptr_eq(&a, &b)` to replace O(N) structural equality checks with O(1) pointer comparisons.
