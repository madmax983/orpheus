## 2025-03-05 - Structural validation fuzzing and f64 bounds overflow
**The Target:** `orpheus-lang` pattern evaluation and type coercion, specifically focusing on bypassing float-to-integer casting boundaries in `eval_positive_integer`.
**The Finding:** Checking extremely large floats directly against integer boundaries (e.g., `i128::MAX as f64`) loses 64-bit precision, rounding the boundary up. Values strictly equal to this inflated float bound pass the constraint but overflow `i128::MAX` when cast, saturating silently and bypassing intended maximum constraints.
**The Action:** Changed bounds checking to use a conservative "soft bound" of `1.0e15_f64` before parsing or casting floats to large integers, guaranteeing safety against precision loss. Additionally, added a `proptest` harness targeting `eval_module` to rigorously fuzz structural parsing logic continuously against invalid and garbage structural inputs.
