## 2025-02-27 - Havoc: MIDI Export OOM
**The Target:** `orpheus-lang::midi_export::export_number_pattern_to_midi` and `orpheus-lang::midi_export::export_sample_pattern_to_midi`
**The Finding:** Calling either of these functions with a massive `cycle_count` (e.g. `1_000_000_000`) caused an enormous vector to be pre-allocated which resulted in a panic (Out of Memory), because the method `Vec::with_capacity(pattern_events.len().saturating_mul(2))` was attempting to allocate RAM linearly proportional to the unconstrained time span size.
**The Action:** Bounded the evaluation time to a maximum of 100,000 steps by checking `cycle_count * steps_per_cycle` against the threshold (similar to how `tracker.rs` limits it), and returning an `EvalError` gracefully rather than attempting to allocate gigabytes of memory.
