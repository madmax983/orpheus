**The Bloat and The Leak**
**Tangle:** The `eval.rs` module in `orpheus-lang` was acting as a God Module, containing AST evaluation logic but also handling audio rendering to WAV files and exporting evaluated events to CSVs. Moreover, these domain-violating functions, along with internal numeric converters like `f64_to_rational` and `eval_into_bindings`, were exposed as `pub fn` to the entire workspace and re-exported in `lib.rs`.
**Blueprint:** Extracted a dedicated `export.rs` module for all side-effecting rendering and file export logic, and moved the `RenderError` type alongside it. Restricted the visibility of internal evaluation utilities (like `f64_to_rational`, `eval_into_bindings`, and `build_span`) to `pub(crate)` or private as appropriate. Updated `lib.rs` and imports to respect the new cleanly separated domain boundary.
