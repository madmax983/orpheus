**🗺️ Atlas: unified error types**
**Tangle:** The `crates/orpheus-lang/src/eval.rs` module contained both evaluation logic and the definition of `EvalError`. Other modules in `crates/orpheus-lang` (`value.rs`, `builtins.rs`) were importing `EvalError` directly from `eval.rs`, creating unnecessary coupling to the evaluation logic just to handle errors. Also, other diagnostic error types (`ParseError`, `TypeError`, `LoadError`) were already centralized in `crates/orpheus-lang/src/diagnostics.rs`.
**Blueprint:** Moved `EvalError` from `eval.rs` to `diagnostics.rs` to centralize all language diagnostic types. Updated `crates/orpheus-lang/src/lib.rs` to export `EvalError` from `diagnostics`. Updated internal imports across the crate to point to the new location, enforcing higher cohesion for errors and lower coupling for the evaluation module.
