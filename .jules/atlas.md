## 2024-05-18 - [Tangle: builtins.rs and eval.rs]
**Tangle:** `builtins.rs` and `eval.rs` had a circular dependency. `eval.rs` imported `builtin_value` and `stack_values` from `builtins.rs` to populate the environment, while `builtins.rs` imported `apply_function_value` and `EvalError` from `eval.rs` to execute dynamic functions (like `jux`).
**Blueprint:** Moved `builtin_value`, `is_sample_identifier`, and `stack_values` (and their associated doctests, refactored as internal unit tests) from `builtins.rs` into `value.rs`. This correctly places environment population alongside the `BuiltinKind` and `Value` definitions they instantiate, breaking the cycle so `eval.rs` no longer depends on `builtins.rs`.
